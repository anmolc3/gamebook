# 🎉 Category F: Party & Social Games

## Overview
Category F contains 14 interactive party games designed specifically to amplify social networking, group laughter, and multiplayer icebreakers.

---

## Social Graph & Ecosystem Integration
Category F games deeply integrate with the social platform layers:
1. **Direct Messages & Chat**:
   - In-chat Game Challenge cards trigger instant lobby entry.
   - Live in-game party chat with typing indicators and expressive reaction pills.
2. **24-Hour Stories**:
   - Funny match outcomes, drawing canvas snapshots, and "Who Am I?" answers can be shared directly to player Stories.
3. **Friend Graph**:
   - One-tap "Invite All Online Friends" button in party lobbies.
   - Mutual friend leaderboards and compatibility percentages.

# 🚀 Product Overview

## Vision
The Social Multiplayer Gaming Platform combines the intimacy and expressive presence of modern social networking (ephemeral stories, real-time messaging, status feeds, and user profiles) with a vast, unified library of 84+ synchronous multiplayer games across 6 core categories (Board Games, Card Games, Casual & Arcade, Fast Competitive, Strategy/Puzzle, and Social Party) in one cohesive mobile app.

---

## Target Audience
- **Casual & Party Gamers**: Seeking instant multiplayer sessions (Tic-Tac-Toe, Ludo, Charades, Would You Rather) with friends and social groups.
- **Card & Strategy Enthusiasts**: Looking for classic board games (Chess, Checkers, Gomoku) and non-gambling virtual card games (UNO-style, Spades, Rummy, Play-Money Blackjack & Poker).
- **Social Circles & Communities**: Wanting a dedicated social home with zero advertising bloat, combining direct chat, 24-hour stories, presence tracking, and one-tap in-chat game invites.
- **Competitive Players**: Players valuing fair, server-authoritative matches, zero-cheat validation, global & friends leaderboards, and persistent lifetime stats.

---

## Core Value Proposition
- **Unified Social-to-Game Loop**: Invite friends directly from 1-on-1 chat or home carousels straight into private or public rooms with zero friction.
- **Extensible Game Engine**: A modular plugin architecture (`GameRegistry` + `GameEngine`) allowing seamless expansion to dozens of game types without rewriting room or networking foundations.
- **Server-Authoritative Fair Play**: Client never dictates dice rolls, card draws, moves, scores, or winners; server validates all moves and prevents cheating.
- **Strict Non-Gambling Policy**: All card and table games operate strictly with virtual play tokens. Zero real money, cash betting, or financial economies.
- **Visual Excellence**: Adapting iOS Marvie design principles with 5 switchable theme families, true Light/Dark modes, zero emoji icons (pure SVG), and smooth 60 FPS mobile performance.
- **Resilient Connectivity**: 30-second disconnect grace periods allowing transparent session recovery during brief network drops.
